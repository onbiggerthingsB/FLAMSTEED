What it didn't do

Over the full 104-match replay, the model and a plain Elo baseline are statistically tied — 0.1561 against 0.1557. It was ahead through the group stage as played live (0.157 against 0.163), and the two converged across the knockouts. Both numbers are published because a forecaster that only reports its good window is not a forecaster.

It has no demonstrated edge against sharp betting markets — its own audit found a statistical tie there too. Nothing here is betting advice, and nothing on this site is sold as one.

For publishers

An embeddable forecast surface for your football coverage — probabilities, not picks.

  • Every number ships with its uncertainty no bare percentages anywhere; a missing input renders as a stated gap, never as a zero
  • Point-in-time by construction a forecast is a read at a timestamp; a result that wasn't knowable then cannot change it
  • Your styling CSS-variable theming, your domain, no cookies and no tracking of your readers
  • A public archive behind every claim each forecast is timestamped and republished; performance claims link to the record

Two tiers — the tournament ladder and schedule, or the full per-fixture detail. Availability by tournament window.
