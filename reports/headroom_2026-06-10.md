## Coverage limitation + G1 recommendation

**Limitation (binding honesty):** the stored 1X2 odds cover exactly **22** matches in four clusters, so the full market-stratified slice table is NOT statistically meaningful (per-slice n would be 2-6). The market gap (Part A) is the aggregate-only headline with a wide CI; the WHERE-is-the-model-weak detail comes from Part B vs realized results, NOT vs the market.

**G1 recommendation:** the measured model–market gap is statistically indistinguishable from
zero (point estimate −0.0201, CI spans 0; audited clean) — i.e. **no measurable market headroom
to chase** on the evidence available. Per the brief's threshold (gap < ~0.005): run **Phase 2a/2b
(+2c as time allows) + Phase 0 daily-loop maintenance**, and **deprioritise Phase 3** — its
premise (closing a model–market information gap) lacks supporting evidence at this sample size.
Caveats for the decision: n=22 is weak evidence either way; Part B shows the model already beats
the Elo reference in essentially every slice (weakest: `provisional=True` teams and the
coin-flip `gap_q=Q1` bucket, which is mostly irreducible). Host-effect calibration (2b) remains
worthwhile independent of the market gap — `host_k=0.5` is an assumption directly driving the
USA/Mexico/Canada 2026 numbers, and the held-out window contains almost no host games to catch it.
