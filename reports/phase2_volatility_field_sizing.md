The **volatility arm** flags a team whose population std of its last `volatility_window=10` rating deltas (inclusive of its final pre-cutoff result) exceeds `provisional_volatility_threshold=16.5` — i.e. erratic recent ratings = low-information even with a long history. It is reported separately from the **few-games arm** (`games < provisional_games=5`), which is essentially irrelevant for a WC field (every qualified nation has played far more than 5 matches). The two arms are mutually exclusive here: a few-games team is never volatility-flagged.
